A Device for Guiding Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation with Real-Time Audio Feedback

App No: 202511060302 Filed 2025

Non-Invasive Medical Device for Treating Lymphedema via Advanced Compression Technology

Dynamic pneumatic compression garment delivering sequential gradient pressure waves to relieve chronic lymphatic fluid build-up in oncology patients.
